Biography
[
]
Brick was the leader of the
Maelstrom
gang in
Night City
. In
2077
, Brick established contact with
Militech
employee
Anthony Gilchrist
using fellow gang member
Ofelia "Patricia" Sirawian
as messenger.
[
3
]
Gilchrist started to secretly collaborate with Maelstrom to provide them with details about Militech convoys in order to steal the shipments and get a cut of the profits after selling the goods.
[
3
]
[
4
]
Brick then made a deal with
fixer
Dexter DeShawn
, who purchased a
Militech MT0D12 "Flathead"
for
€$
10,000 that the gang had stolen earlier.
[
4
]
Although Patricia had warned him of the danger and unpredictability of another Maelstromer called
Royce
, calling him a "feral animal", Brick scoffed at her concerns.
[
3
]
Royce would keep provoking Brick and taunted him to a show-off on the production floor of the
All Foods Plant
.
[
5
]
Eventually Royce deposed Brick before DeShawn could receive his merchandise.
[
3
]
Ellis Carter
was the last open supporter of Brick but turned
cyberpsycho
after the gang forcibly removed some of his implants and beat his face in.

]

Everyone else was simply told Brick had died after Royce took charge.
When
V
and
Jackie Welles
are sent by DeShawn to pick up the purchased Flathead from Maelstrom at the All Foods Factory, Royce is not willing to honor the agreement made by his predecessor Brick and demands an additional payment of
€$
10,000. Depending on the
choices
made during that mission, V can stumble across Brick locked up in a cell and has the option to free, kill, or leave him to rot.
[
4
]
If Brick is freed during The Pickup:
In order to save Brick in
The Pickup
, the detonator device on the table next to his cell door needs to be deactivated. Then, a laptop in the same room needs to be opened, which contains a message "
only you guys know
". This contains the code to Brick's cell door: "9691". If Brick is freed he will thank Jackie and V and promise to return the favour some time. He tells them to leave and that he can manage to escape by himself.
[
4
]
Brick can be encountered later at the
Totentanz
if he is freed by V. Even if Royce was not killed during The Pickup, Brick will have retaken control of Maelstrom, much to the chagrin of his fellow Maelstromers. He is speaking to
Bes Isis
and will thank V for freeing him earlier. Brick will allow them to leave peacefully with Bes.
[
9
]
If Brick is not freed or killed during The Pickup:
Although Brick cannot be directly attacked in his cell, he can be killed either by activating the detonator outside his cell or by triggering the trap manually once inside the cell (cell door code is "9691"). If Royce is also killed during
The Pickup
, it will then be
Ofelia Sirawian
leading Maelstrom in the Totentanz later on. Unless The Pickup was played using the peaceful path, either Maelstrom leader (Ofelia or Royce) will order V to be attacked in the Totentanz.
