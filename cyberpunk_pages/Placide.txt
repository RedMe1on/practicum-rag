Biography
[
]
Early life
[
]
After
Haiti
was decimated by natural disasters, Placide, along with many other Haitians, moved into
Pacifica
, a run-down district of
Night City
. Placide became the second-in-command of the
Voodoo Boys
under
Maman Brigitte
. He radiated an aura of danger and threat – inspiring fear and respect in those around him. Placide cared little for the outsiders who came into Pacifica and would only help those in the Haitian community. Like most of the Voodoo Boys, he was a dedicated netrunner who was determined to uncover the secrets of the
Old Net
.
2077
[
]
In 2077,
NetWatch
had tasked
an agent
to map out the Voodoo Boys data fortress in Pacifica and shut it down.
Ti Neptune
discovered that NetWatch was in their system but was quickly hit with black ICE and frozen, along with Brigitte. The NetWatch agent kept her and Ti in stasis until they were finally awoken after Placide, another member of the Voodoo Boys, hired the mercenary
V
to infiltrate the
Grand Imperial Mall
to stop NetWatch and free Brigitte and Ti.
Placide was V's point of contact with the Voodoo Boys as the latter investigated who tried to kill
Evelyn Parker
with a virus, and in the hopes of finding a way to safely extract the
Relic
. Not knowing or caring what V wanted with Maman Brigitte, Placide intended to use V as a "ranyon", a "floor rag" or disposable merc, to infiltrate the Grand Imperial Mall and figure out what the
Animals
who had recently occupied the place were up to, and if they were connected to attempts to penetrate the Voodoo Boys' private Net, "Reza Agwe". V reluctantly agreed, unaware that Placide was acting independently and knew that Reza Agwe's
attacker
was a member of NetWatch. He installed a surveillance program in V's cyberware that would also act as a lethal virus that would kill the NetWatch agent, Bryce Mosley, every other agent connected to Mosley's network, and ultimately V.
When V discovered NetWatch's involvement, they were perturbed but continued hunting Mosley at Placide's urging.
If V took Bryce's deal in
I Walk the Line
:
Placide was enraged at and suspicious of V's decision. And upon their return to the Voodoo Boys hideout, Placide confronted V as if to start a fight. However, Maman Brigitte interrupted the two, taking V with her. NetWatch agents then attacked Reza Agwe at the moment Alt appeared in the Net, causing her to conflate the Voodoo Boys with NetWatch and burn them. Brigitte and all Voodoo Boys connected to Reza Agwe died, and V was forced to deal with Placide as they left, since he held the access token needed to unlock the church's door.
If V spared Placide in
Transmission
:
Placide would message V later on and threaten them.
If V did not take Bryce's deal in
I Walk the Line
:
Once V hacked into Mosley, Placide ran a virus against the entire NetWatch network. Although, it's unclear if he intended to kill V as collateral-- V burned out but recovered thanks to the Relic. Once confronted, Placide chalked it up as an occupational hazard and blamed Mosely. The situation was deescalated by the awakened Brigitte, who led V to the group's secret mainframe and plugged into the
Net
, in order to retrieve one of
Johnny Silverhand
's memories to use as bait for the VDBs' main project: contacting
Alt Cunningham
beyond the
Blackwall
to negotiate an alliance with the rogue
AIs
there.
If V decided to settle the score in
Transmission
:
V decided to kill the Voodoo Boys to avenge
Evelyn Parker
and/or because of the gang's continued mistreatment of them.
If V spared Placide in
Transmission
:
Placide later messaged V through the Net, telling them to watch their back.
If V decided to leave the Voodoo Boys in
Transmission
:
V ended business with Brigitte peacefully, and left the church under her protection, in which case Placide was somewhat disappointed that he was unable to kill them. Placide would later message V, telling them to watch their back.

Placide
PLACIDE
Placide – one of the top brass in the Voodoo Boys, a secretive and elusive gang of netrunners in Pacifica. The Voodoo Boys normally don't want anything to do with people from the "city," but Mr. Hands managed to arrange a meeting for V with Placide. He's not much of a talker and despises helping outsiders, but if V can use him to get access to Maman Brigitte, the chéfine of the Voodoo Boys, they'll be one step closer to solving their Relic problem.
