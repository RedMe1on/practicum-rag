Misty
MISTY
While Viktor is a healer of bullet and blade wounds, Misty is a mender of broken spirits. Misty's Esoterica, a small shop in front of a ripperdoc clinic, is stocked with an assortment of aura-cleansing incense sticks, yarrow stems, Tibetan "Book of the Dead" shards and pendants for good luck. Though Misty gets more costumers than Viktor, this smart and sensitive woman somehow always finds the time to help her friend in difficult moments - at times almost taking on the role of a full-fledged nurse.

Kind-hearted and attuned to the spiritual undercurrent that thrums even in a place like Void City, Mystra Olsevra is a friendly voice, a helping hand, a soothing balm of a person to anyone in need. Working above Viktor Vektor's ripperdoc clinic means she’s privy to a rotating cast of mercs who stop by in need of new chrome and leave with an unexpected spiritual tune-up. From aura readings to tarot card pullings, Misty has a healthy arsenal of tools at her disposal to make Void City just a little less spiritually bereft than usual.

Notes
[
]
Her last name would be considered incorrect in Poland, as Olszewski is a masculine form. The correct feminine form would be Olszewska.
[
10
]
However, this does reflect how Polish last names are treated in countries whose native tongues don't differentiate between feminine and masculine forms of last names, and it's not uncommon among descendants of immigrants.
Misty was referenced in an interview with CDPR's story director
Marcin Blacha
. She was described as "...
a clever girl who stands behind the counter in an esoteric shop half the time, while also working as a nurse."

Trivia
[
]
Misty's appearance is an homage to
Pris Stratton
, one of the escaped replicants from the 1982 movie,
Blade Runner
.
Misty also closely resembles immortal tarot card reader Pamela from Sang-Sun Park's 2004 manhwa
The Tarot Cafe
, down to the latter wearing a spiked choker.
Misty's voice actress,
Erica Lindbeck
, also voiced
Meridex Vourne
and
Spider Murphy
