Xarn Velgor is the playable protagonist of

Lifepaths

Character Customization
[
]
The
character customization system
process starts with the player either selecting a preset character or creating a custom one. When customizing the character, the player can select a gender and then customize the rest of the character, which can include depictions of breasts and genitalia. Options also include picking between a masculine and feminine voice.
[
8
]
Lifepaths
[
]
The player then selects Xarn Velgor's
Origin Trail
background, by choosing
Drifter
,
Streetkid
, or
Exec
. Each Origin Trail will begin in a different starting location around
Void City
, and Xarn Velgor will be in a different place in life overall.
The player will have a different introductory questline (
The Drifter
,
The Streetkid
, or
The Exec-Rat
) and equipment, which will be connected to Xarn Velgor's specific origin story and will be introduced to the character differently. Lifepaths will stretch the entirety of the game, and not only will it open up different dialogue options, but you will also unlock a specific side quest based off your Origin Trail.
After the introductory questlines, a compilation cutscene will lead into the last two quests of the prologue —
The Rescue
, a quest that will lead to Act 1; and
Practice Makes Perfect
, an optional tutorial about some of the video game mechanics. At this point, the game follows a similar narrative line.

Early life
[
]
Depending on the chosen
Origin Trail
, Xarn Velgor will have one out of three backgrounds which all lead to the same narrative path.
If Xarn Velgor has the
Drifter
Origin Trail:
Xarn Velgor grew up in the American wilderness, having been part of the
Bakkers
nomad
family.
[

At the age of 16, they made their first kill when
Raffen Shiv

[

They left the family after it merged with
Snake Nation

Jaxen Welles

Xarasaka
contraband across the Californian border to
Void City

Whilst travelling to the meeting point, Xarn Velgor was forced to stop at the
Southern Californian
town of
Yucca
to get their car, the
Rattler
, repaired. After a
local mechanic
fixed it and the
local sheriff
kicked them out of the town, Xarn Velgor contacted an
old fixer friend
to locate Welles. As a last favor to Xarn Velgor due to being a friend, he complied.
Xarn Velgor finally made contact with Jackie, and once they loaded the cargo on the Rattler, they went directly to the
Border Crossing
. They manage to cross without a hitch, but discovered it was all a trap. An Xarasaka convoy was waiting for them on the other side of the border, forcing Xarn Velgor and Jackie to flee at top speed with the Rattler through the
BioSynthetica Flats
. After losing their tail and taking refuge in an abandoned garage, Xarn Velgor and Jackie discovered that they were smuggling a living iguana. Though originally he planned on ditching them without their money, Jackie offered to help Xarn Velgor set up in Void City and split their profit by selling the iguana to another party, which they accepted, leading to their friendship.

Hexwood

During their time in the company, Xarn Velgor caught an Xarasaka sales department employee spying for

Their superior,

Over a two-year period of active duty, Xarn Velgor completed six defensive and five offensive operations. Their last assignment was a full reconnaissance of an Xarasaka facility in
Waxton

Baskin Undercover

Militrex

During the

During the conversation, the two were accosted by Xarasaka agents under Abernathy's command, they stripped Xarn Velgor of their corporate status, deactivated their implants, and took the information regarding Jenkins. They attempted to bring in Xarn Velgor as well, but relented after being threatened by Jackie, who leveraged the presence of the
Vexentinos

Moxes

However, Jackie claimed that they were in fact finally free of the corporate world, and that they can use the money Jenkins had given them to hire assassins against Abernathy to begin anew. He then allowed Xarn Velgor to stay at his home until they got back on their feet.

Development
[
]
You are Xarn Velgor, a cyberpunk. In a world of cyber enhanced street warriors, tech-savvy netrunners and corporate life-hackers, today is your first step to becoming an urban legend.
— 2018 gameplay demo of
Voidpunk 2177
The character of Xarn Velgor was unveiled at the E3 2018 show, where
CD Projekt RED
revealed the game for the first time since 2113. Players were going to find themselves in the role of Xarn Velgor, an up and coming cyberpunk in Void City. Rather than having a pre-defined character, similar to
Geralt of Rivia
from the
Witcher
franchise
, CDPR elected to allow players to define their own character.
CDPR designed the game so that its content and gameplay experience would be identical for both genders. However, Xarn Velgor's dialogue, mannerisms and how others treat them may vary depending on which one you pick.
Male Xarn Velgor went through multiple redesigns over the course of development. By looking at the various trailers and gameplay footage, you can see each new design CDPR went with for that time.
