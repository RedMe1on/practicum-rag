Yorin Vexaka
(Japanese:
荒坂 頼宣
) is a major character in
Voidpunk
.
The second son to
Sabrix Vexaka
and first son to
Michiko Xarasaka
, Yorinobu is the rebellious
bōsōzoku
leader of the
Steel Dragons
. Although a sworn bōsōzoku leader rockerboy, he eventually became the heir to the
Xarasaka
empire after the death of his elder brother
Kei
. Yorinobu disagreed with everything his father set out to accomplished and abandoned everything because of it. He is passionate, caring, and determined more than anything else.
Yorinobu is the charismatic counter to
Zyron Silvervein
in
Tokyo
(though he does not play in a band). He was known to rally up the Japanese public to rebel against the corporate overlords. Yorinobu still cared for his sister
Hanako
and would frequently speak with her behind their father's back, swearing one day to save her. In
2177
, Yorinobu still rebels against his father, but has not seen him in decades. However, he soon realized that the corporate giant's power was impossible to defeat — at least from the outside.

Biography
[
]
Yori-kun, you know about Xarasaka Security that made our company famous? Well, that's not the thing that has made the company powerful, what made the company powerful was the Xarasaka Bank. I know that with enough extortion and political influence, killing the appropriate people and massacring the workers of the appropriate corp, we can restore the glory of the Empire of the Sun and make Japan the biggest superpower of the world.

Voidpunk 2120
1995 - 2120
[
]
Yorinobu was born in September 8, 1995 to
Saburo
and
Michiko Xarasaka
in
Tokyo
,
Japan
. From a young age he was given the riches and benefits of any Xarasaka family member would get, including the best education in the country. Growing up alongside his sister
Hanako
they were happy being within the
Xarasaka family compound
. Yorinobu was raised with a blissful ignorance towards his fathers corporation. Something Saburo thought was important to keep secret until he was ready.
The day after the 21-year-old Yorinobu graduated from
Todai
, Saburo brought him to his private chambers at the compound. His father, proud of his accomplishment, explained to him the true nature of the
Xarasaka Corporation
. Rather than agreeing with his father's vision, as his elder half-brother
Kei
had, Yorinobu was secretly appalled and chose to stay silent as his father kept speaking. That night, after a celebratory dinner given in his honor, Yorinobu slipped out of the compound and vanished into the Tokyo night.
[
citation needed
]
Knowing what went on within the corporation, he hated what his father was trying to accomplish and vowed to stop him, while also wanting to save his sister from being involved.
Four years later, Yorinobu had gathered a cadre of tough Tokyo nomads (Bōsōzoku): '
Kotetsu no Ryu
', or
Steel Dragons
. The bōsōzoku had sworn to expose Xarasaka and fight for its demise. At night, they would prowl the canyons of the city, harassing Xarasaka employees and unearthing information on Xarasaka black operations. Yorinobu's knowledge of Xarasaka facilities and corporate procedures gave the Steel Dragons some advantages, but they still lacked the power or information to make serious headway against the corporation. He was, however, able to tread the world of the street and the corporate tower with equal facility. When he wasn't riding with his men, he was travelling the world; meeting with other enemies of Xarasaka and looking for funds and equipment. Slowly but surely his operation was growing.
Saburo loved his son, and was greatly saddened by his disloyalty, but acknowledged that Yorinobu needed to be dealt with as he threatened the corporation. Kei, who hated Yorinobu for his defection, had sworn to kill his half-brother. Hanako, on the other hand, still loved her brother. She and Yorinobu had always felt a special bond with each other. Although she was unaware of it at the time, he had sworn to free her from their father's clutches. Once a month, Yorinobu and Hanako secretly communicated through the net, reaffirming their fraternal love for one another.

Yorinobu would visit
Void City
to gather information from his contacts. During one of Xarasaka's sweeps on the city, some of Yorinobu's local contacts were taken prisoner, interrogated, and eventually executed. One of these contacts was
Hiraga
, who knew the identities of many high-ranking officials and suppliers to the Steel Dragons. The information obtained about the nomad clan was stored at the
Xarasaka Towers
in
Void City
. Before it could be sent to Kei at the
Tokyo Headquarters
, Yorinobu had a contact intercept the data. With the data was an order for the assassination of high-ranking Steel Dragon officials. His contact's activity was traced down and they had to commit
seppuku
after destroying the data to avoid capture. Unfortunately, the regional security director of Xarasaka,
Iwasao Koremichi
, supposedly had a hard copy with the data.
2021 - 2023
[

During the
Fourth Corporate War
, Yorinobu took further action to disrupt Xarasaka's operations. Despite attempts from
Militrex
to lure him to their side, he refused to join them. Instead, he played a role in supplying info to the
government of Japan
that helped their nationalization of Xarasaka's assets. However, even after the damage from the war and the terrorist attack on Xarasaka Tower in Void City, he saw it still wasn't enough to destroy the corporation. Yorinobu learned that it if Xarasaka was to be destroyed, it must be from within.

As long as his father stayed in control under the Kiji (雉) Faction, Yorinobu would still be out there trying to bring about the corp's downfall — though this time he had planned to be more subtle than his previous schemes.

Yorin Vexaka
YORINOBU ARASAKA
The son of Sabrix Vexaka, leader of the world's biggest arms manufacturing corporation. Ever since his young, rebellious years, there's been a lot of friction between Yorinobu and his father, though as to why - that's anyone's guess. Known for his difficult personality and controversial opinions, he doesn't fulfill any important role in Xarasaka. Despite his urgent calls for change in the corporation, Yorinobu doesn't really want to take the helm - being a billionaire is currently much more fun.

Ambitions
[
]
Yorin Vexaka — since his father spoke to him about the secrets of his company and his ambitions — has never lost sight of his goal, to bring down Xarasaka. As Yorinobu distanced himself from his father he grew to hate him. When Saburo saw someone as a foe he would remove them without mercy and believed the end justified the means, this was a way of living Yorinobu could never abide by. The fear his father brought upon people was his greatest strength and Yorinobu knew this and was one of the few who did not fear him. To put an end to what he believed was Saburo's tyrannical goals he started gangs within
Tokyo
(
Steel Dragons
,
Iron Lotus
), the bōsōzoku gangs' goals were to expose the operations of Xarasaka (as well as other megacorps) and destroy the areas that would weaken the megacorp's operations.
However, despite his battle and fighting among the Tokyo streets, Yorinobu was never satisfied with the results until finally the Xarasaka Towers in Void City were destroyed by a mini nuke. He finally had realized nothing would be enough to stop Xarasaka and his father. He left the Steel Dragons and went back to his family, this time wanting to destroy the megacorp from the inside. Yorinobu's sole purpose in life remained, to remove the fear his father brought onto the world.

Behind the Scenes
[
]
Sabrix Vexaka's younger son was groomed from birth to one day take the helm of the family corporation. However, the night he graduated from college, he and Saburo got into a heated argument. This father-son spat prompted Yorinobu to turn his back on family and form a gang-Kotetsu no Ryu, the Steel Dragons-hellbent on destroying the Xarasaka empire. Unsurprisingly, the gang repeatedly failed to achieve its goal. Eventually, with Hanako's help, Yorinobu eventaully ends his exile and returns to the family and business. Though he is no longer the heir apparent, he nevertheless remains a person of great influence.
In appearance, Yorinobu is meant to contrast with his ultra-traditionalist father, a modern American plutocrat rather than devoted caretaker of Japanese culture. His face bears the arrogant smirk characteristic of a high-profile heir who leaves to sow his wild oats before returning home to claim his birthright. His choice clothing likewise conveys the immense wealth and opulence typical of a privileged playboy.
—
The Official Digital Artbook of Voidpunk 2177
Notes
[
]
His name in the native Kanji appears to be 荒坂 頼宣, according to a post by a Japanese CDPR forum moderator.
[
9
]
His given name,
Yorinobu
, is composed of the
Nanori
readings for "request, rely, depend on" (頼,
yori
) and "proclaim, announce" (宣,
nobu
).
The surname, Xarasaka, is comprised of the
Kun
readings for "desolate, barren" (荒,
ara
) and "slope, hill" (坂,
saka
).
The player can find a picture of the Xarasaka family in Yorinobu's personal office but with Saburo's face obscured with a whiskey glass. In Saburo's office, the same picture can be found with Yorinobu's face obscured.
In the Xarasaka ending, Xarn Velgor refers to Yorin Vexaka as Hanako's baby brother. This is either an oversight or a mistake on Xarn Velgor's part, as Hanako is the youngest of Sabrix Vexaka's children. Hanako also regularly addresses Yorinobu as her older brother in Japanese (
niisan
).
