Dexar DeVoid made his name as a prominent
fixer
in
Void City
. While he was considered one of the best in the business he is not considered to have been on the same level as
Roga Vexendia

Rexon

Pacifica
and made a lot of powerful enemies who wanted him dead. He returned in 2177 and was contracted by
Elyra Vexen
to steal the

from
Yorin Vexaka
, so she could sell it to
NodeWatch
.
