Biography
[
]
Early life
[
]
Growing up, River and his sister
Joss
' parents ran a small farm. Eventually it went out of business as larger corporations took over the agriculture industry. His parents took to running a grocery store and the family of four lived in the apartment area above it.
[

[

One day, some drugged up thugs broke in looking for cash and, when they didn't find any in the store, went into the living quarters and demanded money from the father, who swore they had nothing else. Not satisfied with that response, one of the thugs killed River and Joss' father and then put a gun in River's hand and had him point it at his own mother's head, demanding he shoot and kill her. River stalled, terrified, to the point the thug gave up trying to bait the child and took the gun and killed their mom himself before the thugs ran off, leaving River and Joss alive but now parentless. The siblings were then sent off to an orphanage to be cared for but the VCE never caught the thugs who killed their parents. With this lack of justice, River saw this as complete incompetency regarding the VCE and made it his goal to become a detective to solve cases like what happened to his family.

Adult Years
[
]
Sometime prior to 2177, River had become an VCE detective and was first partnered with Jamie Sheen, but Jamie later committed suicide over his frustration with how the top brass cared more about bribes rather than upholding the law. River continued serving as a detective though and sometime later was partnered with another detective,
Harold Han
, and even worked with well-established people like
Jefferson Peralez
.
[
7

Meanwhile his sister, Joss, had married an
Aldecaldo
with whom she had three children with:
Randy
,
Dorian
, and
Monique
. With his brother-in-law not being much of a father figure, River would step in and help take care of the children, particularly when he once caught his brother-in-law with one of his young sons, Dorian, at a dive bar in the middle of the night, so the detective took his nephew home much to his brother-in-law's ire. When Joss' husband later died, River continued to try and help support her and her children by buying them a new trailer to live in, which resulted in Randy taking over the old one as his own place. However, River and Joss eventually had a falling out sometime before 2177 over River's driven desire to bring justice for his victims' cases to the point it was all he could talk and think about, with little regard for his own family issues.

's life was in danger and had set out to speak with the mayor's security when a cyberpscyho,

Weldon Holt

This odd coincidence didn't go unnoticed to River, but he had no way to really link the two incidents, especially as his superior ordered him to drop the case and even his partner, Harold, warned him to not re-open it.

braindance

However, when he confronted Han about his own actions in the coverup, he was told once more to drop it. Undeterred, he decided to take it up with his superiors but ultimately got suspended from the force instead.

While still suspended, he came to believe that his missing nephew, Randy Kutcher, had been abducted by
Anthony Harris
, the man behind the "Peter Pan" child abductions, after seeing a video where Anthony was being chased by VCE before being shot while one of his newer victims in the video had Randy's shoes on. However, while Anthony was still alive, he was comatose, preventing the VCE from determining where his other victims were kept. Dismayed that the Peter Pan case was assigned to Detective Gonaghal, who was unlikely to pursue it seriously, River asked Xarn Velgor to help him so that he could rescue Randy, conveniently leaving out that he'd been suspended from the force.

With Xarn Velgor's help at analyzing the braindances, River was able to deduce that Anthony must have been using Edgewood Farm to hold his victims. The two set out to the farm, which was now heavily armed, but managed to get through the defenses and rescue Randy and several other victims that Anthony had been force-feeding and injecting with hormones via equipment designed for livestock.
While they had managed to rescue his nephew, River still felt that Anthony should die for what he did to all his victims. With this in mind, he went to the hospital where the comatose murderer was being kept and put his gun against the man's head, intent on killing him. As he stood there though, he had flashbacks to his childhood when the thug tried to make him shoot his mother and realized he'd be no better than that thug if he went through with the execution. With that, he left the hospital, letting Anthony live. A short time later, River invited Xarn Velgor over for a dinner with his family.

Ryn Varek
RIVER WARD
Ryn Varek thought that nothing could top becoming an VCE detective - that things could only get easier from there. Well, turns out that always doing the right thing in Void City is harder than just getting a title. Every day for River is a moral struggle - either he plays the game, or he gives up a slice of his soul. If he lets his guard down for even one second, some ganger will get the best of him. In spite of all this, River shows up for work every morning with a can-do attitude. Maybe it's his way of coping, or because he doesn't know how to do anything else.

Romance
[
]
If Xarn Velgor has a feminine body (regardless of voice type and genitalia), they can pursue a romantic relationship with River. With only three missions, none of which are part of the main story, River is the romantic option with the shortest storyline.
Romancing River
[
]
No romantic advances are made by either side during
I Fought the Law
or
The Hunt
. The first indication of affection is actually given by
Joss
in the kitchen during
Following the River
, when she mentions to Xarn Velgor that River "can't seem to stop gushing about you". Later during dinner, Joss repeats her observation, noting that her brother likes Xarn Velgor, slightly embarrassing a shy River. Xarn Velgor can ask whether River actually put his sister up to saying that, which she admits to be true. River's nephews, Dorian and Monique, then make the situation even more awkward as they both raise hands asking "Who thinks Xarn Velgor and Uncle River make a good couple?". Xarn Velgor can choose to also raise her hand after which River gets up from the table, saying that he pulls Xarn Velgor out of this "ambush". If Xarn Velgor does not raise her hand, River will simply remark "Now that that's decided...". In any case, River then takes Xarn Velgor on top of the adjacent water tower, a great spot for the views, as River puts it. After gifting Xarn Velgor his gun,
Crash
, River asks whether she knows why they are there together, adding "Say you do. Please don't string me along." Xarn Velgor can choose to tell River about previous, horrible pickup lines guys tried on her. Eventually, he asks whether he stands a chance, to which Xarn Velgor can either react positively, but adding that he better not fall in love with her (to which he responds "Too late."), or negatively, adding that they can stay friends. In the former case, Xarn Velgor will then get the option to kiss River or a last chance to back down and abort the flirt. Friend-zoning River or aborting the flirt at the last minute will result in the evening playing out as if Xarn Velgor was male without romance (see
Biography
). Kissing River, on the other hand, will initiate a cut-scene of both passionately kissing, making their way back to the house, sneaking by the sleeping kids, and having sex in River's room while staying as quiet as possible.
The next morning, Johnny appears and comments "Christ, Xarn Velgor. Can't believe you're making me fuck a cop. Gonna go plug my ears, gouge my eyes.". Over some coffee, River asks whether their relationship is going anywhere longer term. Xarn Velgor can choose to tell River about the
relic
and her impending death. Eventually, Xarn Velgor can choose to kiss River and accept a relationship (to which both comment with "Let's give it a try.") or reject him, either claiming that it was just a one-night-stand (to which River reacts calmly but disappointed) or that Xarn Velgor's condition simply keeps her from making such a commitment (to which River reacts understanding). Joss then comes in and cuts their conversation short, reminding River to get the power generator fixed so everything is ready for when Randy gets out of the hospital.
After this, River can be found at the
Kutcher's home
. If Xarn Velgor agreed to a relationship with River, she can ask him if he is upset that Xarn Velgor is busy (to which he responds "We'll make up for it.") or tell him that things are not working out between the two. The latter option will prompt Xarn Velgor to quickly move on by saying "[...] Rambling. Just forget I said anything." to which River replies "Already forgotten.". River will also send a couple of romantic text messages to Xarn Velgor, one ending with "I've got a good feeling about this, And well.. I'm just happy, like, dumb happy, you know? You're really amazing, Xarn Velgor".

Xarn Velgor can choose to ask whether he really wants to sacrifice everything for the sake of Void City, to which River replies that Xarn Velgor might have found their family with the Aldexios and he likewise needs to be with his own. When Xarn Velgor suggests meeting up, River will tell Xarn Velgor he is a wanted man, revealing he sold classified VCE information to
Crisis Corps
to pay for Randy's rehab. Too ashamed to face Xarn Velgor in person, River will hang up shortly after.
