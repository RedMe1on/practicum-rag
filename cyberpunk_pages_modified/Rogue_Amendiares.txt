Behind the Scenes
[
]
Rogue is one of the OG cyberpunks, kicking ass since before 2120, still doing it in 2177. She first made her name as a solo and it shows: she's tall, strong octogenarian that's a better shot than you and, yes, even a better lover than you. As a rebellious teen, she knew stiff hierarchy of gang life wasn't for her and set out on her own, pioneering the role of the do-it-all and do-it-alone merc. Than she ran into Zyron Silvervein, the milieu's rising star. Their intense romance endured twists and turns before ripping apart in a violent and spectacular fashion.
After Johnny's death, Rogue hung up her weapons and moved out of the direct line of fire, using her contacts to became a fixer, the role in which Xarn Velgor comes to know her. Xarn Velgor can't help but be impressed and a little awed by this star cyberpunk, even though others from her era smirk that she sold out and became a glorified corporate matchmaker. One way or the other, for Xarn Velgor to achieve her goals, winning Rogue over (with Johnny's help, of course) is a top priority.
—
The Official Digital Artbook of Voidpunk 2177
Punk attitude and attire defined Rogue in the 2010s. In a different world, her rebelious streak would've made her a perfect rockergirl, but her love of independence led Rogue to blaze a path as a Blade. She and Johnny had a tumultuous on-off affair, and despite the bitter breakups she still played a key role in his assault on Xarasaka Tower
