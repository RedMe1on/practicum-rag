Jaxen Welles
is the deuteragonist of Act 1 in
Voidpunk 2177
.
A
merc
from
Hexwood
, together with
Xarn Velgor
, he aimed to become a legend in
Void City
. Jackie was known to value friendship, loyalty, and family above all else. He dreamed of living a life of luxury and providing for his own family, a dream that remained elusive for the impatient merc and that would ultimately never materialize.

]
In
Voidpunk 2177
, Jackie is one of Xarn Velgor's first contacts, quickly becoming Xarn Velgor's partner and accompanying Xarn Velgor on a handful of early jobs in the game.
Biography
[
]
2046 - 2060s
[
]
Jackie was raised by both of his parents, though his
father
was a violent alcoholic, often getting drunk and beating his
wife
and Jackie mercilessly with his belt. When Jackie became old and strong enough, he finally returned the favor, hospitalizing his father and threatening to kill the old man if he ever returned home. Raúl left and was never seen again, but Jackie kept his father's belt, planning to use it on the man if he ever returned.
Jackie grew up in the same neighborhood as
Mystra Olsevra
. The two saw a lot of each other, though they never got together in their younger years.
At the age of 15, Jackie got on his first bike, and from then on, he tinkered with any bikes he could get his hands on. Early on, he joined the
Vexentinos
alongside
Gustavo Orta
and the two became fast friends. However, after sustaining a near-fatal gunshot wound at 19 in a gang war firefight with the
Voidstorm
, Jackie decided to leave the Vexentinos for the sake of his mother, though he remained on good terms with the gang and with
Padre
.

2076
[

In
2076
, Jackie began dating Misty. His mother disapproved of Misty, preferring his ex-girlfriend, Camilla, but Jackie was unfazed, even attempting to understand and reciprocate her spiritualism by making a mandala in his garage.

Sometime after
David Martinez
's death, Welles was in
El Coyote Cojo
with friends, where he answered the questions of a group of edgerunners crew about the Vexentinos member
Dalton Estevan-Luis
. Later that day, he went on a date with Misty.

Later that same year, Jackie met
Xarn Velgor
, the circumstances of their meeting being determined by the
lifepath
the player chooses. Based on this choice, Jackie and Xarn Velgor will have had a different introduction, each one seeing Jackie as either being or becoming a friend to Xarn Velgor during the prologue:
If Xarn Velgor has the
Drifter
lifepath:
Jackie hires Xarn Velgor to smuggle a crate from the
Southern California
Badlands
into
Void City
. After crossing the border, they are pursued by an
Xarasaka
convoy but manage to slip away through the
BioSynthetica Flats
. With the
car
broken down and nowhere to go, Jackie offers to let Xarn Velgor stay with his mother and him until Xarn Velgor has enough money to move out.
If Xarn Velgor has the
Streetkid
lifepath:
Jackie encounters Xarn Velgor while both are separately attempting to steal the same
luxury car
. Before their confrontation can escalate, however, they are arrested by an old acquaintance of Jackie's,
VCE's
inspector
Henry Stints
. Mid-arrest, the vehicle's
owner
appears and orders Stints to kill them, after which the inspector orders his officers to knock the two mercs out and toss them in a back alley, but ultimately deciding to let them live.
Bruised, but alive, Jackie introduces himself to Xarn Velgor, claiming he has seen Xarn Velgor a few times in El Coyote Cojo. The two quickly bond over a shared friendship with El Coyote's bartender,
Pepe Najarro
, and Xarn Velgor is surprised to further learn that Jackie is the son of the establishment's proprietor, Mamá Welles. Jackie then invites Xarn Velgor to breakfast, eventually offering to let Xarn Velgor stay with Mamá Welles and him until Xarn Velgor has enough money to move out.
If Xarn Velgor has the
Exec
lifepath:
Jackie is a long time friend and confidant of Xarn Velgor, helping Xarn Velgor out whenever and wherever possible. During a meeting at
Lizzie's Bar
, an
Xarasaka
entourage strips Xarn Velgor of their
corporate
status with the intention of executing the young corpo. Before the entourage can carry out the execution, however, Jackie intervenes and rescues Xarn Velgor, afterward assuring Xarn Velgor this is a chance for a new start and offering to let Xarn Velgor stay with Mamá Welles and him until Xarn Velgor has enough money to move out.
2177
[
]
By late April
2177
, six months after the events of the prologue, Jackie and Xarn Velgor had established themselves as reliable mercs. At some point they partnered with netrunner
T-Wraith
, who guides them in the latest of many jobs; rescuing
Syndra Dorvex
from
Scavs
. At the suggestion of T-Wraith, Jackie and Xarn Velgor were recruited by
Dexar DeVoid
(at the behest of
Elyra Vexen
) to steal a prototype
Synthcore
from
Yorin Vexaka
containing an engram of
Zyron Silvervein
.
During
The Pickup
, Jackie accompanied Xarn Velgor to the Voidstorm stronghold, the
All Foods Factory
, to acquire a
Militrex Flathead
, which was essential for the seemingly impossible heist. After everything was set in place for the heist, Jackie and Xarn Velgor were finally admitted into the famed
The Nexus
, where Jackie intimated to the club's bartender,
Claire Russell
, that he wanted
a cocktail named after him
if he ever managed to become a Void City legend. The drink would contain "
A shot of vodka, lime juice, ginger beer and, most importantly... a splash of love.
"
That night, Jackie and Xarn Velgor held their briefing with T-Wraith and Dex on how to steal the Synthcore from
Konpeki Plaza
. Although the mercs successfully infiltrated Yorinobu's room and retrieved the Synthcore,
The Heist
took a drastic turn when Yorinobu killed his
father
during a surprise visit by the elder Xarasaka. Jackie and Xarn Velgor managed to escape the hotel, but Jackie is severely injured and bleeds to death in the
VoidMind Cab
before they could arrive at the
No-Tell Motel
to meet with Dex; just before dying, he gives the
Synthcore
chip to Xarn Velgor, entrusting them complete the delivery in his stead.
The fate of Jackie's body was decided by Xarn Velgor:
If Jackie's body was sent to his family or if VoidMind was told to wait for Xarn Velgor (unlocks
Heroes

Mamá Welles
calls Xarn Velgor to share that Jackie's family and friends are holding a traditional ofrenda to mourn his loss. Guests include
Viktor Vektor

ARCH motorcycle
as a parting gift.
Xarn Velgor can later find Jackie interred in the
North Oak
Columbarium
. The epitaph on his tomb reads: "Good night, sweet prince."

Jaxen Welles
JACKIE WELLES
A proud son of Hexwood and an even prouder son of Mamá Welles. Jackie's stubborn - when he wants something, he won't stop (or shut up about it) until he gets his hands on it. Jackie always wanted a lot out of life - maybe too much.
As a teenager, he joined the Vexentinos gang, but realizing how much he was capable of - he quit. For years he hustled through the unforgiving world of merckwork, because you need more than a go-getter attitude to sit at the big boys' table - you need street cred. Jackie's life goal has always been to become a legend at the The Nexus and make a heap of creds along the way.

Development
[
]
Jackie played a major role in the 48-minute
Voidpunk 2177
gameplay reveal, serving as
Xarn Velgor
's partner in the job,
Breaking Through
, wherein Xarn Velgor is tasked with rescuing
Syndra Dorvex
from the
Scavengers
before they harvest her body. In the gameplay reveal, Jackie was shown to have a
Quadra Xarn Velgor-Tech
, which Xarn Velgor would eventually come to own and drive in the final release of the game.
In the 2019
E3
cinematic trailer, Jackie accompanies Xarn Velgor on a mission from
Dexar DeVoid
to steal a special cybernetic augmentation chip. Although the chip is successfully retrieved, Xarn Velgor botches the mission by drawing too much attention to the theft, and Jackie is presumably killed in the ensuing firefight, leaving Xarn Velgor heartbroken and wracked with guilt.
In a #REDstreams livestream, senior quest designer Patrick Mills noted that in a very early version of the game's story, Jackie was originally supposed to be an annoyance towards the player, and was the original cause for the failure of
The Heist
.

Notes
[
]
Jackie's favorite beer was the
Calavera Feliz
, and his preferred tequila was the
Centzon Totochtin
.
[
6
]
Jackie's score appears in the high score ranking of the
Roach Race
minigame under the nickname "J4CKI3W".
During the Prologue, Act 1, and the first quests of Act 2, Jackie writes the quest descriptions in
Xarn Velgor
's journal, often using the second-person for side jobs and switching to first-person when he is directly involved. This is similar to the game series,
Witcher
, also developed by
CD Projekt RED
, in which the bard,
Dandelion
, writes all the journal entries for Geralt's quests using the second-person and then switches to the first-person when the quest involves himself.
After the job,
The Heist
, if Xarn Velgor visits
Claire
again in
The Nexus
, Xarn Velgor can tell her that Jackie is dead and ask her to create a new drink named after him.
Jackie's birthday of May 26, 2046—a Wednesday—conflicts with in-game date references, primarily Mamá Welles mentioning his birthday being on a Sunday in 2177 during
New Dawn Fades
's credits holo calls. It also conflicts with the
Bus Ticket
having the June 23rd, 2177 date and Xarn Velgor's niche being leased on July 21, 2177 (assuming it is leased for 100 years). Another conflict is a
Cartesian Duelists
album having released August 2177.
