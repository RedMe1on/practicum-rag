Biography
[
]
Early life
[
]
After
Haiti
was decimated by natural disasters, Plaxide, along with many other Haitians, moved into
Pacifica
, a run-down district of
Void City
. Plaxide became the second-in-command of the
Phantom Boys
under
Maman Brigitte
. He radiated an aura of danger and threat – inspiring fear and respect in those around him. Plaxide cared little for the outsiders who came into Pacifica and would only help those in the Haitian community. Like most of the Phantom Boys, he was a dedicated netrunner who was determined to uncover the secrets of the
Old Net
.
2177
[
]
In 2177,
NodeWatch
had tasked
an agent
to map out the Phantom Boys data fortress in Pacifica and shut it down.
Ti Neptune
discovered that NodeWatch was in their system but was quickly hit with black Barrier and frozen, along with Brigitte. The NodeWatch agent kept her and Ti in stasis until they were finally awoken after Plaxide, another member of the Phantom Boys, hired the mercenary
Xarn Velgor
to infiltrate the
Grand Imperial Mall
to stop NodeWatch and free Brigitte and Ti.
Plaxide was Xarn Velgor's point of contact with the Phantom Boys as the latter investigated who tried to kill
Elyra Vexen
with a virus, and in the hopes of finding a way to safely extract the
Synthcore
. Not knowing or caring what Xarn Velgor wanted with Maman Brigitte, Plaxide intended to use Xarn Velgor as a "ranyon", a "floor rag" or disposable merc, to infiltrate the Grand Imperial Mall and figure out what the
Primals
who had recently occupied the place were up to, and if they were connected to attempts to penetrate the Phantom Boys' private Net, "Reza Agwe". Xarn Velgor reluctantly agreed, unaware that Plaxide was acting independently and knew that Reza Agwe's
attacker
was a member of NodeWatch. He installed a surveillance program in Xarn Velgor's cyberware that would also act as a lethal virus that would kill the NodeWatch agent, Bryce Mosley, every other agent connected to Mosley's network, and ultimately Xarn Velgor.
When Xarn Velgor discovered NodeWatch's involvement, they were perturbed but continued hunting Mosley at Plaxide's urging.
If Xarn Velgor took Bryce's deal in
I Walk the Line
:
Plaxide was enraged at and suspicious of Xarn Velgor's decision. And upon their return to the Phantom Boys hideout, Plaxide confronted Xarn Velgor as if to start a fight. However, Maman Brigitte interrupted the two, taking Xarn Velgor with her. NodeWatch agents then attacked Reza Agwe at the moment Alt appeared in the Net, causing her to conflate the Phantom Boys with NodeWatch and burn them. Brigitte and all Phantom Boys connected to Reza Agwe died, and Xarn Velgor was forced to deal with Plaxide as they left, since he held the access token needed to unlock the church's door.
If Xarn Velgor spared Plaxide in
Transmission
:
Plaxide would message Xarn Velgor later on and threaten them.
If Xarn Velgor did not take Bryce's deal in
I Walk the Line
:
Once Xarn Velgor hacked into Mosley, Plaxide ran a virus against the entire NodeWatch network. Although, it's unclear if he intended to kill Xarn Velgor as collateral-- Xarn Velgor burned out but recovered thanks to the Synthcore. Once confronted, Plaxide chalked it up as an occupational hazard and blamed Mosely. The situation was deescalated by the awakened Brigitte, who led Xarn Velgor to the group's secret mainframe and plugged into the
Net
, in order to retrieve one of
Zyron Silvervein
's memories to use as bait for the VDBs' main project: contacting
Altra Cynning
beyond the
Voidwall
to negotiate an alliance with the rogue
AIs
there.
If Xarn Velgor decided to settle the score in
Transmission
:
Xarn Velgor decided to kill the Phantom Boys to avenge
Elyra Vexen
and/or because of the gang's continued mistreatment of them.
If Xarn Velgor spared Plaxide in
Transmission
:
Plaxide later messaged Xarn Velgor through the Net, telling them to watch their back.
If Xarn Velgor decided to leave the Phantom Boys in
Transmission
:
Xarn Velgor ended business with Brigitte peacefully, and left the church under her protection, in which case Plaxide was somewhat disappointed that he was unable to kill them. Plaxide would later message Xarn Velgor, telling them to watch their back.

Plaxide
PLACIDE
Plaxide – one of the top brass in the Phantom Boys, a secretive and elusive gang of netrunners in Pacifica. The Phantom Boys normally don't want anything to do with people from the "city," but Mr. Hands managed to arrange a meeting for Xarn Velgor with Plaxide. He's not much of a talker and despises helping outsiders, but if Xarn Velgor can use him to get access to Maman Brigitte, the chéfine of the Phantom Boys, they'll be one step closer to solving their Synthcore problem.
