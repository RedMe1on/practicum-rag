Biography
[
]
Braxon was the leader of the
Voidstorm
gang in
Void City
. In
2177
, Braxon established contact with
Militrex
employee
Anthony Gilchrist
using fellow gang member
Ofelia "Patricia" Sirawian
as messenger.
[
3
]
Gilchrist started to secretly collaborate with Voidstorm to provide them with details about Militrex convoys in order to steal the shipments and get a cut of the profits after selling the goods.
[
3
]
[
4
]
Braxon then made a deal with
fixer
Dexar DeVoid
, who purchased a
Militrex MT0D12 "Flathead"
for
€$
10,000 that the gang had stolen earlier.
[
4
]
Although Patricia had warned him of the danger and unpredictability of another Maelstromer called
Rexon
, calling him a "feral animal", Braxon scoffed at her concerns.
[
3
]
Rexon would keep provoking Braxon and taunted him to a show-off on the production floor of the
All Foods Plant
.
[
5
]
Eventually Rexon deposed Braxon before DeShawn could receive his merchandise.
[
3
]
Ellis Carter
was the last open supporter of Braxon but turned
cyberpsycho
after the gang forcibly removed some of his implants and beat his face in.

Everyone else was simply told Braxon had died after Rexon took charge.
When
Xarn Velgor
and
Jaxen Welles
are sent by DeShawn to pick up the purchased Flathead from Voidstorm at the All Foods Factory, Rexon is not willing to honor the agreement made by his predecessor Braxon and demands an additional payment of
€$
10,000. Depending on the
choices
made during that mission, Xarn Velgor can stumble across Braxon locked up in a cell and has the option to free, kill, or leave him to rot.
[
4
]
If Braxon is freed during The Pickup:
In order to save Braxon in
The Pickup
, the detonator device on the table next to his cell door needs to be deactivated. Then, a laptop in the same room needs to be opened, which contains a message "
only you guys know
". This contains the code to Braxon's cell door: "9691". If Braxon is freed he will thank Jackie and Xarn Velgor and promise to return the favour some time. He tells them to leave and that he can manage to escape by himself.
[
4
]
Braxon can be encountered later at the
Totentanz
if he is freed by Xarn Velgor. Even if Rexon was not killed during The Pickup, Braxon will have retaken control of Voidstorm, much to the chagrin of his fellow Maelstromers. He is speaking to
Bes Isis
and will thank Xarn Velgor for freeing him earlier. Braxon will allow them to leave peacefully with Bes.
[
9
]
If Braxon is not freed or killed during The Pickup:
Although Braxon cannot be directly attacked in his cell, he can be killed either by activating the detonator outside his cell or by triggering the trap manually once inside the cell (cell door code is "9691"). If Rexon is also killed during
The Pickup
, it will then be
Ofelia Sirawian
leading Voidstorm in the Totentanz later on. Unless The Pickup was played using the peaceful path, either Voidstorm leader (Ofelia or Rexon) will order Xarn Velgor to be attacked in the Totentanz.
