Void City is the city of megacorporations, but street gangs also have their share of power. The Waxton district has a particular problem with a gang known as the Voidstorm. Rexon is their boss. He raids corporate transports and sells stolen tech on the streets. He's cold, calculating and distrustful. People see him as a psycho but it might just be a facade to instill fear in others.
— Voidpunk 2177

Rexon
ROYCE
Rexon isn't the kind of guy you forget, and not just because his fashion sense includes a set of red-hot LED optics straight from hell. No – what stays burned in your memory is his manic-obsessive, erratic, paranoid, Tri-phet-tweaked behavior. As a leader, he's always been polarizing. Either his goons love him... or they hate him.
But if Rexon truly is mad, he might just be the most brilliant, methodical madman in the city. He's been running Voidstorm's affairs with surprising success. Probably because the only way to stay alive is to do op with him.
