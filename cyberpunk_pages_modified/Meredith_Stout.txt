in

.
Biography
[
]
Meridex Vourne worked for
Militrex
as a senior operations manager.
[
1
]
In 2177, a company leak led to a Militrex convoy being hijacked by the
Voidstorm
gang and, with the corporation suspecting inside knowledge, tasked Meredith with finding the leak. Shortly after she found evidence leading to her prime suspect,
Anthony Gilchrist
, who was summarily caught and interrogated by her team. However, Meredith did not receive the information she needed or the right amount of evidence. With her job and life on the line, she began to desperately search
Waxton
for any possible leads.
If Xarn Velgor called Meredith and met with her in
The Pickup
:
During this time, Meredith was contacted by a local mercenary named
Xarn Velgor
via holocall and the two decided to meet to discuss the information Xarn Velgor had regarding the stolen convoy from Militrex. However, Meredith suspected Xarn Velgor to be working with Anthony Gilchrist and took the
merc
by surprise and interrogated them with a quick lie-detector test. Once the test revealed Xarn Velgor had nothing to do with her suspect or Militrex, Meredith then decided to discuss terms. Xarn Velgor explained that they knew who had stolen Militrex's convoy and could help lead Meredith to them if she was willing to help. After listening to Xarn Velgor, Meredith decided to work with the mercenary by giving them the money they needed for the purchase of the
Militrex Flathead
and in exchange, she would let Xarn Velgor take the Flathead. This came with one caveat though: Xarn Velgor had to use the money chip she supplied as it had malware installed on it to track the group down, though she conveniently left this detail out when talking to Xarn Velgor.
If Xarn Velgor notified Rexon of Militrex's involvement or refused to take the chip:
After an unsuccessful investigation, Anthony Gilchrist took over the investigation from Meredith and disposed of Meredith's team, replacing them with his own. Meredith was tortured, bound and then thrown into the Waxton waterfront with concrete shoes. After Act 1, her body can be found off a pier in northern Waxton between the Ebunike Docks and Oil Fields fast travel points. Swimming up to the body allows it to be scanned, and
Zyron Silvervein
and Xarn Velgor will have a quick conversation where Xarn Velgor informs Johnny that Stout was from their past life.
If Xarn Velgor followed through with Meredith's plan:
The Militrex shard was able to successfully destroy Voidstorm's systems and gathered all the information she needed. Finally having confirmation of her suspicions of Gilchrist, she had him killed and left his body in Waxton. After the firefight in the
All Food's Plant
, Meredith reached the site with her team to greet Xarn Velgor and their fellow mercenary,
Jaxen Welles
. Meredith acknowledged that she and Xarn Velgor worked well together and maybe in the future they could possibly work together on other assignments. Having the
Drifter lifepath
, Xarn Velgor can also reveal how Gilchrist was compromising Militrex's convoys by finding
his message to Voidstorm
on a computer in a side room of the first larger hall you enter after leaving the initial negotiation room in the All Foods Plant (only as a Drifter you can comprehend the full meaning of the message). You will then be able to relay the information to Meredith, who will be impressed and leave a crate containing Militrex equipment at
Xarn Velgor's Apartment
in Act 2 as an extra reward.
Meredith later appeared on
N54 News
as Militrex's Government Relations Executive during a ceremonial partnership signing between the corporation and the
VCE
at City Hall.
If Xarn Velgor responded to Meredith over text saying "they were starting to like her" or "they wanted to look closer at her ink"  in
Venus in Furs
:
At some point later in Act 2, Meredith messaged Xarn Velgor stating that everything worked out as she had hoped. Despite Meredith not wanting the public to know about what they did, Xarn Velgor replied stating they were fond of her. Meredith, unable to resist, told Xarn Velgor to meet her at the
No-Tell Motel
. The two later met there for some intimate alone time.
