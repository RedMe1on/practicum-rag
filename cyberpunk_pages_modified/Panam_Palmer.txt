Biography
[
]
Vexa Morrigan was part of the Aldexios nomad tribe. Her best friend was captured by the
Raffen Shiv
, a hostile Drifter group, when they were a child, only to be saved by fellow-Aldecaldo
Cassidy Righter
- an incident that deeply affected Panam into her adulthood, to the point of never telling anyone what happened in order to forget about it. Cassidy would remain a close friend of Panam and become somewhat protective of her.

Panam also befriended several other clan members, including
Mitch Anderson
and
Driss "Scorpion" Meriana
. Panam idolized
Saul Bright
, a fellow Aldecaldo who used to pull off impressive as well as funny stunts in his earlier years.
[

At some time, Panam and Mitch were hauling cargo from
San Francisco
to
Kansas
for the owner of a
syn-meat
farm. The stench at the farm got Panam so sick that she puked on the owner's boots to which he simply replied "No need to bow, I'll just take a handshake."

Another "long story with many twists and turns" involved the corpse of the Deputy Finance Director of
Kaukaz

Badlands
outside
Void City
. She didn't see eye to eye with the clan's leader, Saul Bright, who was trying to strike a deal with
BioSynthetica
in his attempt to keep the clan alive with consistent work. Panam disapproved, believing the clan should remain independent from corporations. Together with Scorpion, Panam kept joking about getting Mitch on board and heading off to
Oregon

Panam's conflict with Saul came to a head, however, when, due to her rebellious attitude, she eventually left the clan and moved to Void City, even though her friend Mitch tried to talk her out of it at first.

Raffen Shiv

Despite moving away, Panam maintained communication with members of the Aldecados, including her close friends Mitch Anderson and Scorpion.

Kryon Tech

If Xarn Velgor assists Panam in clearing the wreckage:
Xarn Velgor and Panam wiped out the surviving Kryon Tech forces around the downed Aero-Void and found Mitch inside, held at gunpoint by a guard. After Xarn Velgor or Panam killed the guard, or convinced him to back off, they head to the gas station to the west, where Hellman and his bodyguards took shelter. After Hellman's capture, Aldexios reinforcements appear outside and Panam and Xarn Velgor part ways. Panam's subsequent text messages to Xarn Velgor reflect uncertainty about her life outside the clan and Xarn Velgor can give her the advice to rejoin them.
If Xarn Velgor abandons Panam and heads straight for the gas station:
Xarn Velgor left Panam and single-handedly retrieved Hellman from a nearby gas station that he and his bodyguards sought refuge in. Panam then showed up with Aldexios reinforcements, saying that she managed to rescue Mitch herself and that unlike Xarn Velgor, she does not bail. Her subsequent text messages will be very frustrated about her life outside the clan and include a remark about Mitch, who gave Scorpion an Aldexios-style funeral by himself.

Some time later, Saul Bright disappeared with a small party of Aldecados. Learning of the attack, Panam returned to the Aldecados to help investigate. Eventually, they discovered the group had been ambushed by
Wraiths

As the plan is covert infiltration, only Xarn Velgor and Panam set out, with the latter remaining at distance to provide the former with sniper cover fire if needed.

Starting the job to rescue Saul but then leaving the area for too long will result in Panam sending a message saying that Saul is dead, and the job will be failed, precluding all follow-up missions involving Panam.

If Xarn Velgor successfully helps to rescue Saul:
After Xarn Velgor rescued Saul, Panam drove the group away, escaping from pursuing Wraiths and using a sand storm to cover their tracks. Seeking shelter for the night in an abandoned farmhouse. A grateful Saul invites Panam to rejoin the Aldexios, but insists she will follow orders in the future. She responds by strongly objecting to Saul's planned cooperation with BioSynthetica. As Saul turns in early, Panam sarcastically asks whether he needs "help"... to which Saul just responds "Fuck off" and remarks that sandstorms are called "haboobs" in north Africa - a word he is very fond of. As fellow Aldexios
Cassidy Righter

Overwatch

Behind Saul's back, she is determined to proceed anyway and gathers some like-minded fellow Aldexios including Mitch as well as Carol Emeka, and also contacts Xarn Velgor to join her at the Drifter camp.

If Xarn Velgor betrays Panam's plan to Saul:
If Xarn Velgor approaches Saul instead of Panam and tells him about her plan, the mission is aborted. A few hours later, a furious Panam contacts Xarn Velgor, saying that her, Mitch, and Carol are now being treated like lepers by the others. She ends their friendship and severs all ties with Xarn Velgor, precluding all follow-up missions involving Panam. Saul will subsequently contact Xarn Velgor, saying that both he and Panam are grateful, although Panam might not realize it yet, and gifts Xarn Velgor a

If Xarn Velgor helps Panam to steal the Basilisk:
If Xarn Velgor does not tell Saul about Panam's plan and instead immediately joins her and her group, the mission proceeds as planned with Xarn Velgor's assistance. They decide to block the Militrex convoy's path using an abandoned
train system

Upon returning to the camp, Panam has another heated argument with Saul, who feared Militrex could trace its stolen merchandise back to the Aldexios and told her they would have a "family council" soon to decide Panam's future in the clan. Cassidy then had the idea to, after unloading, leave the large, stolen convoy trucks in front of the Wraith camp Saul was held at previously in order to shift the blame for the heist. Panam and Xarn Velgor then part ways as she and the others prepare to assemble the Basilisk.

The next day, an enthusiastic Panam calls Xarn Velgor, asking to take the newly assembled Basilisk for a spin together. After returning to the camp, Cassidy, Mitch, and Panam tell Xarn Velgor that they left the implicating loot from the raid on Militrex near a Raffen Shiv camp to shift the blame. Panam and Xarn Velgor then hop into the tank after Mitch makes some last minute adjustments. Panam instructs Xarn Velgor to sync in to practice movement and target shooting with the tank's neural impulse interface outside of the camp. After that, Panam shows Xarn Velgor the dual interface by joining them in the neural connection with the Basilisk, thereby linking their nervous systems and sharing their sensory awareness. If Xarn Velgor is male, Panam will then use the interface to make sexual advances, which Xarn Velgor can accept and thereby initiate the romance storyline (see

After the Raffen Shiv have been fought off, Saul wants to talk to Panam, who then expects to be kicked out of the clan for her foolhardiness. Instead, however, Saul makes Panam the new co-leader of the clan by his side, admitting he was wrong earlier, mistaking her willingness to risk everything for the family with recklessness. Saul and surprised Panam then decide to move the camp to a new location for safety reasons. To cool down, Panam shares a drink with Xarn Velgor at the camp's bar and reiterates that they can always count on the Aldexios for help. She also asks Xarn Velgor to join the clan, who has to decline in order to "finish some things first". After passing out from a

On the way, Saul, Mitch, and Cassidy will express their relief of seeing Xarn Velgor back up on their feet. Anders Hellman

Vexa Morrigan
PANAM PALMER
Panam is a nomad - although, maybe "ex-nomad" rings truer these days. She locked horns with the family's leader, Saul, left Aldecaldo nation and moved to Void City to try her luck as a merc. Panam's life is at a crossroads - on one hand she knows her life needs a change, it's now or never. On the other hand, burning bridges with the nomad family where all her friends are might have been a bit drastic. On top of that, Void City doesn't take too kindly to nomads or have patience for people like Panam who are internally conflicted and dream of a brighter future.

Romancing Panam
[
]
If Xarn Velgor has a masculine body (regardless of voice type and genitalia), they can pursue a romantic relationship with Panam. As such, Panam is unique as she is the only potential romantic partner for Xarn Velgor, who can also be called for help during
Nocturne Op55N1
towards the end of the game.
Optional Prelude
[
]
Xarn Velgor can begin to flirt with Panam in the
Sunset Motel bar
at the end of
Ghost Town
, suggesting that they only get one room together. Although it will upset her initially, not completing the optional objectives earlier to assist Panam against the Raffen Shiv and Nash makes no difference. Panam agrees to sharing a room as that would save them money, pointing out that there are twin beds in every room. If Xarn Velgor pursues the matter further, Panam first plays naive and then makes clear that they can "celebrate"... but not today. In their joint room, Panam will just immediately go to sleep in full clothes.
Xarn Velgor can resume flirting in the abandoned farmhouse at the end of
Riders on the Storm
, after Saul went to bed. Although Panam will reject Xarn Velgor touching her thigh, they can offer to fulfill her any wish, after which she replies "Careful, you can't know what I'd wish for..." and puts her legs on Xarn Velgor's lap. Both fall asleep and
Zyron Silvervein
chimes in, wishing them sweet dreams. As Panam is about to depart the next morning, Xarn Velgor has the option to ask her about last night, to which Panam replies that she is not that kind of girl. After Xarn Velgor asks just what kind of girl she is, she responds by pulling him in for a kiss. Johnny then appears again, saying Xarn Velgor should ride right after her if circumstances were different, but then and there they can just look. Johnny moves on saying "Damn hard to catch a girl like that. Only hope you've got is they feel a pang and stop to wait."
During
With a Little Help from My Friends
, Xarn Velgor finds some time with Panam alone in the control tower and can choose to tell her that he missed her pretty bad. Panam immediately gets uncomfortable, as she is not used to talking about feelings. If Xarn Velgor presses the issue, Panam admits that she also wants to talk about "it", but moves on by saying "My plan was to tiptoe around, test the waters, then retreat back into my shell." She adds that her hesitance is motivated by a fear to cock things up due to her volatile behavior and that, with Xarn Velgor, she does not want to act on impulses but prefers to play it safe because - this time - she really cares. Later at the campfire with the other nomads, Xarn Velgor can scooch closer to Panam and put his arm around her while looking at the stars together.
Not initiating any of the advances described above has no effect on the following romance option with Panam.
