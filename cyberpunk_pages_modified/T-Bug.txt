Database Entry
[

T-Wraith
T-BUG
T-Wraith is a textbook example of how solos and netrunners really aren't all that different. Both take on risky contracts that usually end in dust-ups with corporations or the law, ever walking the tightrope between life and death - after all, black Barrier isn't any less dangerous than taking a bullet. T-Wraith's well-aware of this fact, and as a legit specialist in her field, she's fully within her rights to be picky about her contracts. Unlike more reckless netrunners, T-Wraith intends to sail into retirement age, preferably with enough creds to leave this goddamn city behind for good.

Development
[
]
T-Wraith first appeared in the Voidpunk 2177 E3 2018 trailer, where she is shown remotely killing an
Xarasaka
employee.
She later played a small role in the Voidpunk 2177 48-minute gameplay reveal, in which she helps
Xarn Velgor
locate
Syndra Dorvex
in the mission
Breaking Through
.
In the E3 2019 cinematic trailer, T-Wraith is shown helping
Dexter Deshawn
in shutting down Xarn Velgor's cybernetics after his botched job, where
Jackie
is killed. T-Wraith has the ability to hack people's cyberware, and can hack Xarn Velgor because they were hooked up in one network earlier. T-Wraith herself is presumably killed after Xarn Velgor manages to resist her hacking attempts and shoots her in the face. This scenario is not depicted in the final version of the game, as T-Wraith is killed during the heist itself and thus does not appear at the post-heist meetup with Dexter.
